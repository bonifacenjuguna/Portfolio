04 / About

Still becoming the engineer I want to be.

I'm a Computer Science student who learns best by turning ideas into working systems.

That has led me through Telegram bots, backend services, APIs, authentication, databases, internet measurement and automation. I'm now deliberately strengthening the fundamentals underneath those projects — especially cybersecurity and networking.

I care about understanding what happens beneath the interface: how requests move, how data is stored, how permissions are enforced, how systems fail and how to make them more reliable.

I'm not presenting the learning journey as finished. This portfolio is part of it.
